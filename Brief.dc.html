BUILD BRIEF · DAY 3 · 45 MINUTES HydroMind is the coordination and decision layer between farm intelligence and farm actions. HydroMind هو طبقة التنسيق واتخاذ القرار بين ذكاء المزرعة وإجراءات المزرعة.
CORE STORY

Separate systems can make locally correct decisions that conflict at the farm level. HydroMind coordinates them into one system-level decision and explains why.

الأنظمة المنفصلة قد تتخذ قرارات صحيحة محليًا لكنها تتعارض على مستوى المزرعة. HydroMind ينسّقها في قرار واحد على مستوى النظام، ويشرح السبب.

PROTOTYPE HYPOTHESIS

When an operator sees two individually reasonable requests create a system-level conflict, they should understand the value of a coordination layer without first needing an explanation of multi-agent AI.

THE SCENARIO — MISHKAT FARM, JEDDAH

Four-zone NFT rig, shared 100 L reservoir, butterhead lettuce, hot afternoon. A realistic operating scenario built from the farm's operating ranges — not a recorded historical event.

pH 6.42 IN RANGE 5.5 – 6.5
EC 2.41 HIGH 1.80 ± 0.10
LEVEL 62 % LOW 70 – 90 %
TEMP 24.6 HIGH 18 – 22 °C
MODE NORMAL NO BOUND CROSSED

Three readings are outside their operating targets. pH is not one of them. Say it that way.

DILUTION ESTIMATE — AN APPROXIMATION, NOT MASS CONSERVATION
C₁V₁ ≈ C₂V₂
2.41 × 62 ≈ 1.80 × V₂
V₂ ≈ 83.0 L → fresh water ≈ 21.0 L
Assumes temperature-compensated EC and approximately linear dilution. Float/overflow cutoff is 95 %, not 100 %. At 95 L the same estimate gives EC ≈ 1.57 — illustrative only, never presented as measured.
